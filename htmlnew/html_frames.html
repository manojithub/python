13. Frames — FRAMESET, FRAME and Target

Understand the legacy frame concepts required by the textbook.

Important: Frames are obsolete in modern HTML, but they remain in this tutorial because they are explicitly included in the Plus Two textbook syllabus.

FRAMESET

FRAMESET divides the browser window into rows or columns. The textbook notes that a frameset page does not need a BODY section.

<FRAMESET Cols="200,*">
  <FRAME Src="menu.html" Name="left_frame">
  <FRAME Name="right_frame">
</FRAMESET>

Rows

<FRAMESET Rows="20%,30%,50%">
  <FRAME Src="top.html">
  <FRAME Src="middle.html">
  <FRAME Src="bottom.html">
</FRAMESET>

Targeting

<A Href="bio.html" Target="right_frame">Biography</A>

The linked page opens inside the frame whose Name is right_frame.

Nested frameset

<FRAMESET Rows="85,*">
  <FRAME Src="header.html">
  <FRAMESET Cols="200,*">
    <FRAME Src="menu.html">
    <FRAME Src="content.html">
  </FRAMESET>
</FRAMESET>

NOFRAMES

<NOFRAMES>Your browser does not support frames.</NOFRAMES>
