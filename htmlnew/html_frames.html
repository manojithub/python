5.5 Dividing the Browser Window — Frames

FRAMESET, FRAME, targeting, nested frames and NOFRAMES.

Frames are included because they are explicitly taught in the textbook and used in its examination exercises. Modern HTML no longer recommends framesets, but they remain relevant to this syllabus.

FRAMESET

<FRAMESET Rows="20%,30%,50%">
<FRAME Src="sampleframe1.html">
<FRAME Src="sampleframe2.html">
<FRAME Src="sampleframe3.html">
</FRAMESET>
FRAMESETUse
ColsVertical divisions
RowsHorizontal divisions
BorderBorder thickness
BordercolorBorder colour

FRAME attributes

Src, Scrolling, Noresize, Marginwidth, Marginheight and Name are discussed in the textbook.

Targeting

<A Href="bio.html" Target="right_frame">Biography</A>

NOFRAMES

<NOFRAMES>Your browser does not support frames.</NOFRAMES>
