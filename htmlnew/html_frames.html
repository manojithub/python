13. Frames – FRAMESET, FRAME, Target and NOFRAMES

Detailed textbook-based explanation of frames, attributes, targeting and nested framesets.

Important: FRAMESET/FRAME are legacy HTML features, but they are included here because they are explicitly taught and assessed in the textbook.

FRAMESET

FRAMESET partitions the browser window into frames. A frameset page does not use a normal BODY section.

Tag/AttributePurposeExample
FRAMESET RowsDivides window horizontally into rows<FRAMESET Rows="20%,80%">
FRAMESET ColsDivides window vertically into columns<FRAMESET Cols="200,*">
FRAMEDisplays a page inside a frame<FRAME Src="main.html">
SrcPage displayed in frameSrc="home.html"
NameNames a frame for Target linksName="right_frame"
TargetA link attribute used to open a page in a named frameTarget="right_frame"
NOFRAMESAlternative content for browsers not supporting framesContainer tag

Nested framesets

A frameset can contain another frameset, allowing a row to be divided into columns or vice versa.
