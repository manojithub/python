15. HTML5 – New Tags and Multimedia

Detailed explanation of the HTML5 tags explicitly introduced in the textbook.

What is HTML5?

The textbook describes HTML5 as a major revision of HTML and highlights multimedia and new structural elements.

HTML5 TagPurposeSimple example
VIDEODisplays video<VIDEO Controls>...</VIDEO>
AUDIOPlays audio<AUDIO Controls>...</AUDIO>
CANVASArea for graphics/drawing through scripting<CANVAS></CANVAS>
HEADERIntroductory/header content for a page or section<HEADER>...</HEADER>
FOOTERFooter information for a page or section<FOOTER>...</FOOTER>
ARTICLESelf-contained article/content<ARTICLE>...</ARTICLE>
SECTIONThematic section of content<SECTION>...</SECTION>

AttributePurpose
ControlsShows playback controls
AutoplayAttempts automatic playback
