15. HTML5 Overview

Study the HTML5 features and new elements listed in the textbook.

HTML5

The textbook describes HTML5 as a major revision after HTML 4.01 and mentions features such as video playback and drag-and-drop.

New tags listed in the textbook

TagPurpose
VIDEOProvides video playback.
AUDIOProvides audio.
CANVASDrawing graphics, charts and graphs.
HEADERHeader for a document or section.
FOOTERFooter for a document or section.
ARTICLEIndependent stand-alone content.
SECTIONGroups content into subject areas.

Example

<!DOCTYPE html>
<HTML>
<HEAD><TITLE>HTML5 Page</TITLE></HEAD>
<BODY>
<HEADER><H1>My Website</H1></HEADER>
<SECTION>
  <ARTICLE>Welcome to my website.</ARTICLE>
</SECTION>
<FOOTER>Copyright</FOOTER>
</BODY>
</HTML>
Study strategy: Learn the traditional HTML tags for the board examination and understand the HTML5 additions as the newer development of web structure and multimedia.
