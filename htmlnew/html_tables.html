5.4 Creating Tables

TABLE, TR, TH, TD, attributes, spanning cells and captions.

Simple table

<TABLE Border="1">
<TR>
<TH>Roll No</TH><TH>Name</TH>
</TR>
<TR>
<TD>1</TD><TD>Aliya</TD>
</TR>
</TABLE>
Tag / AttributePurpose
TABLETable container
TRRow
THHeading cell
TDData cell
BorderBorder thickness
CellspacingSpace between cells
CellpaddingSpace between content and border
RowspanVertical cell spanning
ColspanHorizontal cell spanning
CAPTIONTable caption

Spanning and caption

<TH Colspan="2">TOTAL Patients</TH>
<TD Rowspan="2">Example</TD>
<CAPTION>Student Details</CAPTION>
