4. Headings, Paragraphs, BR and HR

Detailed notes on H1-H6, P, BR and HR including the textbook attributes and examples.

H1–H6 Headings

There are six heading levels. H1 is the highest/largest heading and H6 is the smallest.

TagUseAttribute covered
H1–H6Display headings of six levelsAlign: left, center, right (as used in textbook examples)
PCreates a paragraphAlign: left, right, center, justify
BRMoves following content to a new lineNo closing tag
HRDraws a horizontal ruleWidth, Size, Align, Noshade

HR attributes

Width controls width, Size controls thickness, Align controls position,Noshade removes the shaded appearance and color changes the colour of horizontal rule.
