4. Headings, Paragraphs, BR and HR

Learn H1-H6, P, BR and HR with syntax, attributes and examples.

Headings H1 to H6

The textbook provides six heading levels. H1 is the largest and H6 is the smallest.

<H1 Align="left">Heading 1</H1>
<H2 Align="center">Heading 2</H2>
<H3 Align="right">Heading 3</H3>
<H4>Heading 4</H4>
<H5>Heading 5</H5>
<H6>Heading 6</H6>

P — Paragraph

P organises text into paragraphs. The textbook describes Align values left, right, center and justify.

<P Align="justify">HTML helps us organise information into web pages.</P>

BR — Line break

BR moves the following text to a new line.

First line<BR>Second line

HR — Horizontal rule

<HR>
<HR Width="60%" Size="3" Align="center">
Remember: H1-H6 are heading tags. BR and HR are empty tags in the textbook treatment.
