10. Multimedia – Music and Video

Learn EMBED, NOEMBED and HTML5 AUDIO/VIDEO with attributes and examples.

EMBED and NOEMBED

The textbook introduces EMBED for embedding multimedia content and NOEMBED for alternative content when embedding is not supported.

Tag/AttributePurpose
EMBEDEmbeds multimedia such as audio/video in textbook examples
SRCSpecifies the media file/source
WIDTHSpecifies embedded width
HEIGHTSpecifies embedded height
NOEMBEDProvides alternative content for unsupported embedding

HTML5 AUDIO and VIDEO

HTML5 provides dedicated audio and video elements. Common attributes used in teaching examples include controls, autoplay, loop, muted, width and height.

AttributeMeaning
controlsShows playback controls
autoplayAttempts to start automatically
loopRepeats media
mutedStarts media muted
widthVideo display width
heightVideo display height
