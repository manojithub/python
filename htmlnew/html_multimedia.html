10. Audio and Video — EMBED

Learn the multimedia method presented in the textbook and its attributes.

EMBED

The textbook introduces EMBED for providing audio and video in web pages.

<EMBED Src="song1.mp3" Width="300" Height="60">
</EMBED>
AttributePurpose
SrcMedia file/location.
WidthDisplay width.
HeightDisplay height.
AlignAlignment.
AltAlternative information.
HiddenControls visibility.

NOEMBED

<EMBED Src="music.mp3">
<NOEMBED>Your browser does not support embedded media.</NOEMBED>
Modern note: The textbook teaches EMBED. HTML5 also provides dedicated AUDIO and VIDEO elements.
