Images and Presentation Tags

IMG, PRE, ADDRESS, MARQUEE, DIV and FONT.

IMG — inserting images

<IMG Src="picture1.jpg">

Src identifies the image file. The textbook also discusses Width, Height, Vspace and Hspace.

PRE — preformatted text
