5. Text Formatting Tags

Master B, I, U, S, BIG, SMALL, STRONG, EM, SUB, SUP, BLOCKQUOTE and Q.

Basic formatting

TagPurposeSyntax
BBold<B>text</B>
IItalic<I>text</I>
UUnderline<U>text</U>
S / STRIKEStrike-through<S>text</S>
BIGLarger text<BIG>text</BIG>
SMALLSmaller text<SMALL>text</SMALL>
STRONGStrong importance<STRONG>text</STRONG>
EMEmphasis<EM>text</EM>

Subscript and superscript

H<SUB>2</SUB>O
H<SUB>2</SUB>SO<SUB>4</SUB>
(a+b)<SUP>2</SUP> = a<SUP>2</SUP> + 2ab + b<SUP>2</SUP>

Quotations

BLOCKQUOTE is used for longer indented quotations. Q is intended for short quotations.

<BLOCKQUOTE>Mother nature needs care and protection.</BLOCKQUOTE>
<Q>Keep your world clean and green.</Q>
