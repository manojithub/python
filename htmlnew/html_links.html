PLUS TWO COMPUTER SCIENCE

5.2 Creating Links

Internal, external, graphical and e-mail hyperlinks.

Anchor tag

<A Href="https://example.com">Visit Website</A>

The A tag creates hyperlinks and Href gives the target address.

Internal linking

<A Name="Introduction">INTRODUCTION</A>
<A Href="#Introduction">Go to Introduction</A>

E-mail hyperlink

<A Href="mailto:slmanojkumar@gmail.com">Email Manojithub</A>

Graphical hyperlink

<A Href="page2.html">
<IMG Src="button.jpg">
</A>
Exam focus: Know Href, Name and Target, and differentiate internal and external hyperlinks.
