Common HTML Tags & Text Formatting

Headings, paragraphs, breaks and textbook formatting tags.

Headings

HTML provides six heading levels from <H1> to <H6>. H1 is the largest and H6 the smallest. The textbook also discusses Align.

<H1 Align="left">Heading 1</H1>
<H2 Align="center">Heading 2</H2>
<H3 Align="right">Heading 3</H3>

Paragraphs and breaks

<P>This is a paragraph.</P>
