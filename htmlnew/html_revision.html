19. HTML Revision

Fast revision of tags, attributes, important differences and exam points for Plus Two students.

One-page revision map

TopicRemember
StructureHTML → HEAD/TITLE + BODY
Container tagOpening + closing tag
Empty tagNo closing tag; BR, HR, IMG are textbook examples
TextH1-H6, P, BR, HR
FormattingB, I, U, S/STRIKE, BIG, SMALL, STRONG, EM, SUB, SUP, BLOCKQUOTE, Q, PRE, ADDRESS, MARQUEE, DIV, FONT
ImageIMG: Src, Alt, Width, Height, Vspace, Hspace
ListsUL, OL, LI, DL, DT, DD; Type/Start where applicable
LinksA: Href, Name, Target
MultimediaEMBED, NOEMBED, AUDIO, VIDEO
TablesTABLE, TR, TH, TD, CAPTION; table attributes
FramesFRAMESET, FRAME, NOFRAMES; Rows/Cols, Src/Name, Target
FormsFORM, INPUT, TEXTAREA, SELECT, OPTION, FIELDSET, LEGEND
HTML5VIDEO, AUDIO, CANVAS, HEADER, FOOTER, ARTICLE, SECTION

Must-remember differences

PairDifference
UL vs OLUL uses bullets; OL uses ordered numbering/letters.
Radio vs CheckboxRadio is normally used to choose one from a group; checkbox allows multiple selections.
Cellspacing vs CellpaddingBetween cells vs inside cell around content.
Text vs TextareaSingle-line control vs multi-line control.
Action vs MethodAction specifies processing destination; Method specifies GET/POST.
FRAMESET vs FRAMEFRAMESET divides the window; FRAME displays a document inside a frame.
Final exam tip: Practise attributes along with tags. In HTML questions, marks are often lost by remembering the tag but forgetting the required attribute or its value.
