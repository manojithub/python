12. Table Attributes – Complete Reference

Detailed explanation of every table attribute covered in the textbook, with examples and exam notes.

Complete table attribute reference

AttributeUsed withExplanation
BorderTABLEControls border thickness in textbook examples.
BordercolorTABLESpecifies border colour in textbook HTML.
AlignTABLEControls horizontal alignment such as left/center/right in textbook examples.
BgcolorTABLE/TD/THSets background colour.
BackgroundTABLESpecifies background image.
CellspacingTABLESpace between adjacent cells.
CellpaddingTABLESpace between cell content and its border.
WidthTABLETable width.
HeightTABLETable height.
RowspanTD/THNumber of rows a cell spans.
ColspanTD/THNumber of columns a cell spans.
ValignTD/THVertical alignment of cell content.
Very important: Cellspacing is the space between cells; cellpadding is the space inside a cell around its content. This distinction is frequently tested.
